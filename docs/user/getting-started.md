# Getting Started – User Guide

This guide walks you through creating your account, setting up your profile, and taking your first actions on NETTRADES.AI.

### Launcher Login

To access the full Launcher functionality, log in with your Odoo credentials:

1. Open the NETTRADES Launcher
2. Click **Login** in the top-right corner
3. Enter your Odoo username and password
4. Your session is stored securely for subsequent API calls

### Switching Operational Modes

After logging in, you can switch operational modes from the Launcher dashboard:

1. Click the **Operational Mode** section in the Launcher
2. Select one of the three modes:
   - **🔴 100% Sovereign AI** – All inference stays on local GPUs
   - **🟡 Hybrid Mode** – Local first, fallback to marketplace
   - **🟢 Cloud First** – Local → Marketplace → External APIs

The mode change takes effect immediately.

---

## 1. Creating Your Account in the NETTTRADES.AI Hub 

1. Go to [NETTRADES.AI](https://nettrades.ai) and click **Sign Up**.
2. Choose your account type:
   - **Company** – If you're hiring or posting projects
   - **Freelancer** – If you're offering services
   - **Job Seeker** – If you're looking for employment
   - **Researcher** – If you're seeking research partners
3. Enter your email and password.
4. Verify your email address (check your inbox).
5. Complete your profile – we'll guide you through it.

> **💡 Tip:** You can also sign up using your **LinkedIn** or **GitHub** account for faster onboarding.

---

## 2. Completing Your Profile

Your profile determines how the AI matches you with opportunities. The more complete your profile, the better the matches.

### For Job Seekers

1. Upload your CV (PDF or DOCX). The AI will automatically extract:
   - Skills
   - Work experience
   - Education
   - Summary
2. Add your LinkedIn or GitHub profile.
3. Set your preferred job types (remote, hybrid, on-site).
4. Add your desired salary range.

### For Freelancers

1. Complete your skills and portfolio.
2. Set your hourly rate.
3. Add examples of your work.
4. Set your availability (Full-Time, Part-Time, Unavailable).

### For Companies

1. Add your company details (name, logo, website).
2. Set your hiring preferences.
3. Create your company profile.

> **💡 Tip:** Your profile completeness is shown as a percentage. Aim for 100% to get the best AI matches.

---

## 3. Posting a Job

As a company, you can post job openings to attract candidates.

1. Go to **Jobs** → **Post a Job**.
2. Fill in the job details:
   - **Job Title** – Clear, descriptive title
   - **Description** – Detailed role description
   - **Required Skills** – List the key skills needed
   - **Location** – Remote, hybrid, or office location
   - **Salary Range** – Budget for the role
3. Click **Post Job**.
4. The AI will immediately start matching candidates.

> **💡 Tip:** Use clear, specific language in your job description. The AI matches based on keywords and context.

---

## 4. Finding Candidates (AI Matching)

Once your job is posted, the AI automatically finds suitable candidates.

1. Go to your job posting.
2. Click the **Match Candidates** button.
3. The AI analyses all applicants and ranks them by relevance.
4. Review the ranked list – each candidate shows:
   - Match score (0-100%)
   - AI-generated reasoning for the match
   - Skills comparison
5. Shortlist candidates or invite them for an interview.

---

## 5. Getting Expert Help ("Ask Someone")

Need a second opinion or specialised knowledge?

1. Click the **Ask Someone** button next to any AI-generated answer.
2. Describe your question or upload the artifact (e.g., code, document, analysis).
3. The AI infers the professional field and matches you with experts.
4. Choose from the top 5 recommended experts.
5. The session starts with funds held in escrow.
6. Chat with the expert in real-time.
7. Rate the expert when the session completes.

> **💡 Tip:** Your questions are private. Only the expert sees your question. If you enable "Use Expert Answers for Training", only the expert's answer is used to train the AI – your question is never stored.

---

## 6. Sharing or Renting GPUs

### Sharing Your GPU

If you have idle GPU capacity:

1. Go to **GPU Manager** → **Share GPU**.
2. Download the GPU agent installer.
3. Run the installer on your GPU machine.
4. The agent auto-detects your GPU and registers it with the network.
5. Set your sharing schedule (e.g., only at night).
6. Start earning tokens!

> **💡 Tip:** You can continue using your GPU while sharing – the agent runs in the background.

### Renting GPU Capacity

If you need GPU power for inference or fine-tuning:

1. Go to **GPU Manager** → **Rent GPU**.
2. Choose your requirements:
   - GPU type (NVIDIA, AMD, etc.)
   - Memory required
   - Duration
3. The AI finds the best available GPU in the network.
4. Pay with tokens or with your credit card.
5. Your job runs on the distributed network.

---

## 7. Voting on Answers ("Good Answer")

When you see a helpful answer (AI-generated or human):

1. Click the **Good Answer** button.
2. The answerer's reputation increases.
3. If you're a qualified professional, your vote carries more weight.
4. Votes on AI answers feed into the fine-tuning pipeline – you're helping improve the AI!

---

## 8. Exploring the Dashboard

Your dashboard gives you an overview of:

- **Jobs** – Your posted jobs and applications
- **Projects** – Your active projects
- **Notifications** – Real-time updates
- **GPU Usage** – Your GPU sharing status and earnings
- **Reputation** – Your reputation points per field
