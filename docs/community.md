# Community

Welcome to the NETTRADES.AI community! This page lists all the ways you can connect with other users, contributors, and maintainers.

---

## Why Join the Community?

- **Get help** – Ask questions and get answers from experienced users
- **Share knowledge** – Help others and build your reputation
- **Contribute** – Report bugs, suggest features, or submit code
- **Stay updated** – Learn about new releases and features
- **Network** – Connect with other professionals in the space

---

## Community Channels

### 💬 Discord (Real-Time Chat)

The fastest way to get help and connect with the community.

**👉 [Join our Discord](https://discord.gg/nettrades)**

| Channel | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| `#general` | General discussion |
| `#help` | Get help with the platform |
| `#contributing` | Discussion about contributing |
| `#development` | Technical discussion for developers |
| `#announcements` | Release announcements |

## Community Guidelines

1. **Be respectful** – Treat others with kindness and respect.
2. **Be helpful** – Help others when you can.
3. **Be constructive** – Provide constructive feedback.
4. **Be inclusive** – Welcome everyone, regardless of background.

We follow the [Code of Conduct](https://nettrades.github.io/nettrades-platform/governance/code-of-conduct/).

## Recognition

### Contributors

Contributors are recognised on GitHub and in release notes.

### Community Heroes

Exceptional community members may be recognised as Community Heroes.

### Maintainers

Long-term contributors may be invited to become maintainers.
