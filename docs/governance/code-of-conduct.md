# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Be respectful** – Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- **Be empathetic** – Consider other perspectives and experiences.
- **Be constructive** – Provide helpful feedback, not criticism.
- **Be collaborative** – Work together to solve problems.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- **Harassment** – Offensive comments, threats, or intimidation.
- **Discrimination** – Exclusion based on personal attributes.
- **Trolling** – Deliberately provoking or disrupting discussions.
- **Doxxing** – Publishing private information without consent.
- **Unwelcome advances** – Sexual attention or advances.

---

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behaviour and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behaviour.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for other behaviours that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (including GitHub, Discord, and the community forum) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project team at [conduct@nettrades.ai](mailto:conduct@nettrades.ai). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter of an incident.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

| Incident | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **First violation** | Private warning, with explanation of the violation and expected behaviour. |
| **Second violation** | Public warning and temporary ban from the community. |
| **Third violation** | Permanent ban from the community. |
