# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NETTRADES.AI collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our platform. By using NETTRADES.AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## Overview

NETTRADES.AI is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect only the data necessary to provide our services and we handle it with care. This policy covers:

- What data we collect
- How we use your data
- How we store and protect your data
- Your rights regarding your data

---

## Data We Collect

### 1. Account Data

When you create an account, we collect:

- **Email address** – used for login, notifications, and password recovery.
- **Name** – to personalise your profile and communications.
- **User type** – Company, Freelancer, Job Seeker, Researcher, or Expert.
- **Password** – stored securely using industry‑standard hashing.

### 2. Profile Data

You may optionally provide:

- Skills, experience, education, portfolio, hourly rate, availability.
- Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Upwork, and other professional profiles.
- CV/resume files (PDF, DOCX).
- Profile photo.
- Geolocation (optional) – used for proximity matching in "Ask Someone".

### 3. Usage Data

We collect data about your interactions:

- Job postings and applications.
- Project proposals and milestones.
- "Ask Someone" sessions and messages.
- GPU sharing and rental activity.
- Votes on "Good Answer".
- Token balance and transactions.

### 4. Technical Data

We collect limited technical data for security and performance:

- IP address (for analytics and fraud prevention).
- Browser type and version (for compatibility).
- Device information (operating system, screen size).

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only when necessary:

| Recipient | Data Shared | Purpose |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Stripe** | Payment details | Processing payments and escrow |
| **GPU providers** | Anonymised job specs (only when you rent a GPU) | GPU rental |
| **Experts** | Your question (for "Ask Someone" sessions) | Expert consultation |
| **Legal authorities** | As required by law | Compliance |

---

## Data Storage and Security

- **Data is encrypted** in transit (TLS) and at rest (database encryption).
- **Passwords** are hashed using bcrypt.
- **API keys and secrets** are stored encrypted.
- **Access is restricted** to authorised personnel only.
- **Regular security audits** are conducted.

For companies running the platform on their own infrastructure, data never leaves their control.

---

## Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| Account data | Until account deletion |
| Profile data | Until account deletion |
| Session data | 30 days |
| Usage logs | 90 days (anonymised after) |
| Messages | 30 days after session ends |
| Token transactions | 7 years (for audit) |

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** – request a copy of your data.
- **Rectify** – correct inaccurate data.
- **Erasure** – request deletion of your data.
- **Restrict** – limit how your data is used.
- **Portability** – request your data in a structured format.
- **Object** – object to certain uses of your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at [privacy@nettrades.ai](mailto:privacy@nettrades.ai).
